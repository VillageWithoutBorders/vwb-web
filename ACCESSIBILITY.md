# Accessibility

Village Without Borders is for everyone. That includes people who read slowly or not at all, people who use screen readers, people with shaky hands or low vision, and people on old phones with weak signal.

## What we aim for

- **Plain words.** Short sentences anyone can follow. Spanish is built in.
- **Read to me.** The app can read pages out loud.
- **Phones first.** Every tool works on a small phone screen, and buttons are big enough to tap.
- **Big text works.** Pages still work when text is made twice as large.
- **Easy to see.** Strong contrast between text and background, in both light and dark mode.
- **Keyboard and screen reader friendly.** Labels on buttons and fields, and a visible outline on whatever you're on.
- **Less motion.** If your phone asks for reduced motion, the app keeps still.
- **Weak signal.** Many pages still open with no signal, and messages wait to send until you're back online.

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. An automatic check for the WCAG 2.1 AA rules runs on every change to the code.

## What we know isn't there yet

- Some admin tools are easier on a computer than a phone.
- The Spanish has not had a full read by a native speaker yet.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something in the app is hard for you to use, we want to know.

- In the app: Help, then send us a message.
- By email: **info@villagewithoutborders.org**
- On GitHub: open an issue and choose **Something isn't working**.

Tell us what you were trying to do, what got in the way, and what phone or computer (and any helper tools, like a screen reader) you use. You don't need the technical words.

# Helping with Village Without Borders

Thank you for wanting to help. Every village is built by many hands.

## Ways to help

- **Report a problem.** Open an issue and tell us what happened, what you expected, and what phone or computer you used.
- **Suggest an idea.** Open an issue and tell us who it would help and how.
- **Fix or build something.** Read the steps below first.

## Before you write code

- Open an issue first for anything bigger than a small fix, so we can talk it through together.
- Keep it plain and kind. The app is for people of all reading levels, ages, and abilities.
- Privacy comes first. Never show someone's address, and keep locations rounded.
- Build for phones first. Buttons should be easy to tap, and text should be easy to read.

## Sending your changes

1. Copy (fork) the repository and make a new branch.
2. Make your change and test it with `npm run dev`.
3. Sign off on each commit with `git commit -s`. This adds a line like:

   `Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>`

   It means you wrote the change, or have the right to share it, and you agree it comes in under this project's licenses. (This is called the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org).)
4. Open a pull request and say what you changed and why.

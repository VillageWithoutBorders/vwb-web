# Keeping Village Without Borders Safe

Village Without Borders holds information about real neighbors. We take their safety seriously.

## Found a security problem?

Please **do not** post it in a public issue. That could put people at risk before it is fixed.

Instead, report it privately:

1. Go to the **Security** tab of this repository.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Tell us what you found, how to see it happen, and what could go wrong.

We will reply as soon as we can and keep you updated while we fix it. Once it is fixed, we are glad to thank you by name if you want.

## What counts

- Someone could see private messages, locations, or account details they should not see
- Someone could sign in as another person or change their account
- Someone could get around blocks, admin limits, or privacy settings
- Secret keys or passwords showing up in the code

## Running your own village?

If you run your own copy of this app, you are responsible for keeping your own server, database, and keys safe. Never put secret keys in the code.

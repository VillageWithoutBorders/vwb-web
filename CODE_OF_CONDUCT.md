# How We Treat Each Other

Village Without Borders is built by neighbors, for neighbors. This page is for everyone who helps with the project here: people who report problems, suggest ideas, write code, draw art, or translate.

## What we ask of you

- **Be kind.** Talk to people the way you would want a neighbor to talk to you.
- **Be patient.** People here have all kinds of reading levels, abilities, languages, and skill levels. Explain things plainly. Nobody has to know the right words to belong here.
- **Disagree about ideas, not people.** It's fine to say "I think this could work better another way." It's not fine to put someone down.
- **Protect privacy.** Never share someone's address, location, messages, or personal details. Not here, not anywhere.
- **Give credit.** Say thank you, and name the people whose work helped you.

## What is not okay

- Hate or put-downs about who someone is: race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age, money, where they come from, or anything else
- Harassment, threats, or following someone around to bother them
- Sexual comments or pictures
- Sharing anyone's private information
- Spam, or trying to sell things
- Pushing people to take a political side. VWB is for everyone.

## If something goes wrong

Email **info@villagewithoutborders.org**. Tell us what happened, where, and when. Links or screenshots help.

We will read every report and keep your name private. We may:

- talk with the person
- remove comments, issues, or code
- block someone from the project, for a while or for good

If you report a problem in good faith, you will never be in trouble for it.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you speak for Village Without Borders.

The app has its own Community Guidelines for neighbors using it. This page is for the people building it.
